# sun2023: Sun, Victor et al. 2023, Cell, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2023.08.037, PMID 37774678

**Source used:** full text of the NIH author manuscript PMC10644954, which is the accepted, unedited manuscript. I checked the PMID by title in Europe PMC ("Human microglial state dynamics in Alzheimer's disease progression", Cell 2023, Sun N, Victor MB, Park YP, ...). The Europe PMC `fullTextXML` endpoint (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10644954/fullTextXML) returned HTTP 500 on every try. I therefore took the same PMC XML from NCBI E-utilities (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pmc&id=10644954). A local copy is at `scratchpad/ad/fulltext/PMC10644954.ncbi.xml`, with a text version beside it. I read the Summary, the Highlights, all Results subsections, the Discussion and Limitations, the STAR Methods and all main and supplementary figure legends. I did not open Table S1, a multi-page xlsx with metadata, markers, DEGs and TWAS genes; it is linked from the data website. Every quote was checked by script against the full text.

**Data**
- Cohort: ROSMAP (Religious Orders Study and Rush Memory and Aging Project).
- Donors: 443 (226 non-AD, 217 AD; of the AD donors, 138 are early AD and 79 late AD).
- The groups come from k-means clustering on 7 z-scored pathology and cognition variables (global AD pathology, amyloid, tangles, global cognition and others), labelled no / low / high AD pathology. In the text these are "non-AD / early AD / late AD", and in the open metadata column `ADdiag3types` they are nonAD / earlyAD / lateAD. The authors note that this "does not conform to standard diagnostic criteria".
- Regions and donors per region:
  - Prefrontal cortex: 423 donors. The composition tests use the PFC only.
  - Mid-temporal cortex, angular gyrus, entorhinal cortex, thalamus and hippocampus: subsets of 48-65 donors (plus 21 more), as described in the Methods.
  - The paper says "prefrontal cortex". I coded it `DLPFC` to match the other ROSMAP claim files (mathys2019, green2024), because ROSMAP PFC tissue is dorsolateral. Claims using pooled regions are coded `multiple`.
- Assays:
  - snRNA-seq with 10x 3' v3, about 2.8 M nuclei across all cell types. In-silico sorting kept 174,420 immune nuclei, of which 152,459 are microglia.
  - snATAC-seq (10x ATAC v1): 41,832 microglia from about 100 donors (92 PFC plus 48 multi-region).
  - 10x multiome: 4,244 microglia from 19 donors.
  - Validation: IHC and RNAscope (including HiPlex) on PFC. These use 4 AD vs 4 non-AD donors, 4 AD donors only for HiPlex, and 8 donors for IHC.
- Data access, **controlled for raw data / open for processed data**:
  - Raw human data and full ROSMAP metadata: Synapse **syn52293417** (AD Knowledge Portal, Data Use Certificate). Metadata are also at syn3157322 or the RADC Hub.
  - Processed, deidentified immune-cell counts (`ROSMAP.ImmuneCells.6regions.snRNAseq.counts.rds`, 632 MB) and per-cell metadata are open at https://personal.broadinstitute.org/cboix/sun_victor_et_al_data/, linked from http://compbio.mit.edu/microglia_states/. I looked at the metadata header. It has brainRegion, batch, age_death, msex, pmi, ADdiag3types, seurat_clusters (the state) and a deidentified subject. The same folder has the snATAC ArchR object, the cluster DEGs and the supplementary tables.
  - The open metadata do not include pathology scores. Claims C05-C07 need the controlled ROSMAP metadata.
  - UCSC Cell Browser: rosmap-ad-aging-brain / microglia-states.
  - iMGL (iPSC microglia) bulk and snRNA-seq: GEO **GSE227223** (open).
  - Code: github.com/nasunmit/scMicroglia; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.8297901.
  - The data are not on CELLxGENE: there was no match by DOI in the curation API.
- Reuse: the human snRNA-seq is the MIT/ROSMAP data that also underlies other Tsai/Kellis papers (Mathys et al.; refs 11, 15, 16 for the in-silico sorting). The website links a participant-overlap table between MIT ROSMAP studies. Its claims are therefore not independent of other ROSMAP claim files.

**Claims left out**
- iPSC-microglia (iMGL) experiments, which are in vitro only:
  - The pulse-chase: MG10 markers respond acutely; MG2 and MG8 markers stay active; amyloid fibrils induce IL1B early and LRRK2 late without CPEB4.
  - The iMGL snRNA-seq: 18,914 nuclei, 10 clusters, no homeostatic MG0 counterpart; LPS lowers P2RY12 and blunts ATP calcium responses.
  - The overexpression screen of 25 homeostatic TFs: 9 raise calcium transients, and RUNX2 and PPARG reduce ameboid morphology.
  - CRISPRi of FOXO3, HIF1A and FOXP2: blocks LPS induction of IL1B and the MG10 signature, without gaining homeostatic markers.
- Regional composition: all 12 states are present in all six regions, but their proportions differ significantly between regions (Fig. S2A-C). This is not an AD contrast.
- Covariate effects on state proportions: age (MG4, MG5), PMI (MG1, MG4) and sex (MG3) (Fig. S2K). These are not AD claims, but are relevant as covariates.
- Human-tissue TF validation: PPARG and RUNX2 levels in IBA1+ cells correlate with TMEM119, while SMAD3, IRF8 and ELF1 do not (Fig. 4H-I, Fig. S6F-G). This has no AD contrast.
- snATAC details: the accessibility of P2RY12, CCL3 and SPP1 in the HOM/ACT1/ACT2 states, GREAT functions of the state peaks, and the 13-TF co-regulatory module of MG0. Summarised in the C15 and C16 notes only.
- APOE/PPARG RNAscope substates (88% of PPARG+ nuclei are APOE-high; 27.6% of APOE+ nuclei are PPARG-low). Folded into C11.
- Group G1 (up in both stages) and the other 45 DEG groups are in Fig. S8 / Table S1 with no named genes in the text.
- MAGMA associations with lupus, ulcerative colitis, triglycerides and other traits (Fig. S10A). Not AD.
- The 7 multi-category regulators other than BIN1 and RELB (APOE, PICALM, THRB, ZBTB16, HS3ST1). They are listed in the gene_source of C29.

**Ambiguities and caveats**
- **Stage contrasts:** "late response" compares late AD with early AD, not with non-AD. Two-phase groups (G49, G50) are coded with direction NA and the phase spelled out in `comparison`.
- **DEG criteria:** a gene counts as a DEG if it passes in at least 2 of 3 methods: MAST (cells), MAST with a random effect, and edgeR pseudobulk (donors). Covariates are age, sex, PMI, race, batch, region and LBD/PD. Regions are pooled for DE, and composition is PFC-only.
- **Composition tests:** propeller with covariates. The paper does not say which pairwise contrast (early or late vs non-AD) carries each composition change. Fig. 1C shows *** and Fig. S2D has the table.
- **RNAscope (C08-C10):** 4 vs 4 donors. The legend gives "n=91 to 149 cells per group" and a Student's t-test, so it is unclear whether donors or cells were the unit. The text calls the P2RY12 result only "relatively lower".
- **Fig. 1G marker label:** the legend calls LRRK2/FOXP1 "MG2 markers". The text and Fig. 2 make them MG8 markers, so C10 is coded as MG8.
- **DAM signature:** the Results say the DAM signature is strongest in MG3, driven by ribosomal protein genes. The Discussion says DAM signatures are lowly enriched in all states "with the only exception being the homeostatic state MG0". C11 follows the Results.
- **Figure citation for C14:** the MG10 early vs MG2/MG8 late DE statement cites Fig. S4E-H, which are iMGL qPCR panels. Fig. 5A, with DEG counts per state, is the likely intended source.
- **Garbled TF name:** one sentence ("Several TFs, like ` in inflammatory MG8") lost a TF name in the manuscript XML.
- **Sample counts:** they differ slightly between sections, for example snATAC "100 individuals" in the Results versus "92 PFC + 48" in the Methods, and the "194k" total combines RNA and ATAC microglia.
