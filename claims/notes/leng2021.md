# leng2021: Leng et al. 2021, Nat Neurosci, doi:10.1038/s41593-020-00764-7, PMID 33432193

**Source used:** full text of the NIH author manuscript PMC7854528. I checked the PMID by title in Europe PMC ("Molecular characterization of selectively vulnerable neurons in Alzheimer's disease", Nature Neuroscience 2021, Leng K, Li E, ...). The Europe PMC `fullTextXML` endpoint (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7854528/fullTextXML) returned HTTP 500 on every try. I therefore took the same PMC XML from NCBI E-utilities (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pmc&id=7854528). A local copy is at `scratchpad/ad/fulltext/PMC7854528.ncbi.xml`, with a text version beside it. I read the abstract, all Results subsections, the Discussion, the Methods (statistics, pseudobulk DE, re-analysis of Mathys et al.), Table 1, and the legends of the main and Extended Data figures. I did not open the Supplementary Tables (1: DEGs of vulnerable neurons; 2: Braak 6 vs 0 DEGs). Every quote was checked by script against the full text.

**Data**
- Cohort: UCSF Neurodegenerative Disease Brain Bank and the Brazilian BioBank for Aging Studies (Sao Paulo). Cases were selected to have no non-AD pathology: no Lewy bodies, TDP-43, primary tauopathy or cerebrovascular change. Argyrophilic grain disease was allowed.
- snRNA-seq donors: 10, all male and all APOE e3/e3. Every donor contributed both regions.
  - Braak 0: n = 3 (ADNC A0-1/B0/C0, CDR 0-0.5).
  - Braak 2: n = 4 (low ADNC, CDR 0-2).
  - Braak 6: n = 3 (A3/B3/C3, CDR 3).
  - In the inventory I count the 3 Braak 6 donors as AD and the 3 Braak 0 donors as controls. The 4 Braak 2 donors are early-stage tau donors and are named only in the n_pd text.
- Regions: caudal entorhinal cortex at the mid-uncus (`EC`), and superior frontal gyrus at the level of the anterior commissure, Brodmann area 8 (`SFG`).
- Assay: 10x Chromium 3' v2 snRNA-seq on OptiPrep-isolated nuclei. Cell Ranger 2.1 with a pre-mRNA GRCh38 reference.
- Nuclei after QC: 106,136 (42,528 EC; 63,608 SFG), about 10,000 per donor.
- Validation cohort: multiplex immunofluorescence (TBR1, RORB, CP13 phospho-tau, DAPI) on 26 donors across Braak 0-6 (Braak 0-1 n = 6, 2-4 n = 12, 5-6 n = 8). This cohort includes females and APOE e4 carriers, and 5 of its donors overlap the snRNA set.
- Re-analysis: Mathys et al. 2019 (ROSMAP prefrontal cortex, Synapse syn18485175), analysed per sex with 12 AD and 12 control donors in each. Leng calls the region "prefrontal cortex". The source samples are Brodmann area 10, so I coded them `DLPFC`, as for mathys2019.
- Data access, **open**:
  - GEO **GSE147528**: raw reads and unfiltered UMI matrices.
  - Synapse **syn21788402**: post-QC single-cell data.
  - CELLxGENE collection **180bff9c-c8a5-4539-b13b-ddbc00d643e6** (I found it via the CELLxGENE curation API by DOI). It should carry the authors' cell-type labels; I did not check whether the subpopulation labels (EC:Exc.s2 etc.) are included.
  - Code: kampmannlab.ucsf.edu/ad-brain-analysis.

**Claims left out**
- Methods and quality statements: transcript coverage compared with Mathys and Grubman, and clustering with and without cross-sample alignment (Extended Data Fig. 1).
- SFG:Exc.s7 decreased significantly at Braak 2 but not at Braak 6. The authors call the change "negligibly small", so it is mentioned only in the C13 notes.
- Female Mathys:Exc.s1 trend (P_unadjusted = 0.17). Also Marinaro et al. (familial AD) reporting vulnerable RORB+ subpopulations ExcB1/ExcB4: that is another group's data, cited only as support.
- Morphology: RORB+ EC layer II neurons include both pyramidal and large multipolar ("stellate") neurons, and some large multipolar neurons are RORB- (Fig. 3h). This is a qualitative imaging observation with no AD contrast. The authors conclude that "morphology alone is insufficient".
- Microglia subclustering (4 EC and 5 SFG subpopulations) could not resolve DAM, because most DAM and homeostatic markers were barely detected in nuclei (Extended Data Fig. 8). This is a negative technical result. Only the microgliosis trend is kept, as C20.
- Discussion hypotheses: RORB-driven gene programs cause vulnerability, and inhibitory neurons change in morphology or function rather than number.
- No mouse or iPSC experiments in this paper. Mouse data are used only as references: medial EC layer genes from Ramsden et al., and spinal-cord-injury reactive astrocyte DEGs from Anderson et al.

**Ambiguities and caveats**
- **Power:** 3 vs 4 vs 3 donors. Composition is tested by beta regression on per-donor proportions with Braak stage as the covariate, and Holm correction within each family. Several composition claims are trends that do not survive correction (C01, C02, C13, C20). The text often gives no exact adjusted P for the significant ones (C03-C05, C07); the figures show them.
- **Relative abundance:** subpopulation proportions are within the cell type (for example within EC excitatory neurons). The authors themselves read the rise of EC:Exc.s5 (C07) as a compositional effect.
- **Braak DE (C11, C12):** the test for Braak 6 vs 0 DE is not described in Methods. Only the subpopulation-vs-rest pseudobulk edgeR is described, and I assumed the same approach was used.
- **Marker names:** CTC-340A15.2 and CTC-535M15.2 are old GENCODE/Ensembl clone names for lncRNAs and need mapping to current symbols before a re-test.
- **Typos kept in the quotes:** the text writes "EC:Exc.7" for EC:Exc.s7, and "did not any detect" in the quote of C18.
- **Mathys:Astro numbering:** the text says Mathys:Astro.s2 matches GFAPhigh astrocytes in its markers, while the DEG overlap is reported for Mathys:Astro.s3 (C26). The paper does not resolve this.
- **No AD contrast for glial states:** no change with Braak stage is claimed for the GFAPhigh astrocytes or the AD-associated oligodendrocyte subpopulations. These claims (C21-C26) are state-existence and state-signature claims.
- **Fisher test (C17):** the phospho-tau test pools cells across donors.
