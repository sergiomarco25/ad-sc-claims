# lau2020: Lau et al. 2020, PNAS, doi:10.1073/pnas.2008762117, PMID 32989152

**Source used:** Europe PMC full-text XML for PMC7568283 (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7568283/fullTextXML). PMID checked by title in Europe PMC and PubMed. I read the Significance statement, the abstract, all Results subsections, the Fig. 1-5 legends, the Discussion and the Methods. The SI Appendix (Figs. S1-S7, Tables S1-S5) was not opened, so per-gene statistics and subpopulation proportions are not in the claims.

**Data**
- Prefrontal cortex: "BA6, BA8, and BA9 domains". Coded as `PFC`, not `DLPFC`, because BA6 (premotor) and BA8 are mixed in.
- Source: South West Dementia Brain Bank (Brains for Dementia Research).
- Donors:
  - AD: 12 (8 male, 4 female), Braak >= 4, DSM-5 clinical AD.
  - Control (NC): 9 (6 male, 3 female), Braak <= 2.
  - Mean age is 74.6 y for AD and 85.4 y for NC, so the controls are about 11 years older.
  - Other neurodegenerative, vascular and systemic diseases were excluded.
  - APOE genotype was measured (TaqMan) but not used in any analysis.
- Assay: snRNA-seq with 10x Chromium 3' v3 on NovaSeq 6000. Cell Ranger 3.0.1 (pre-mRNA reference), then Seurat v3 integration (21 samples).
- Nuclei: 169,496 in total (90,713 AD, 78,783 NC) and 29,171 genes. Six cell classes: astrocytes 11.9%, endothelial 2.3%, excitatory 45.2%, inhibitory 14.1%, microglia 4.7%, oligodendrocytes 21.8%. There is no separate OPC class.
- Data: GEO **GSE157827**, open, with MTX/TSV matrices for 21 samples. GEO also links PMID 37117777, a later paper that reuses the data. I did not check whether author cell-type or subcluster labels are deposited.

**Claims left out**
- Atlas statements: 43 clusters, six classes and the "novel" markers (ADGRV1, GPC5, RYR3, ABCB1, EBF1, CBLN2, LDB2, LHFPL3, PCDH15, LRMDA, DOCK8, PLP1, ST18).
- Sample quality against postmortem delay.
- Sex-stratified concordance with the bulk microarray (SI Fig. S3). It is bulk only and described only qualitatively.
- Astrocyte subpopulations a2, a4, a5, a7, a8 and a9 were "similar" between groups. This is a minor null result.
- Microglial subpopulations m1 and m7 are AD-associated, but no direction or genes are given in the text.
- Mouse-only results: endothelial reprogramming in amyloid and tau mouse microarrays (SI Fig. S7E). It is presented as conservation, not as a human single-nucleus claim.
- Speculation in the Discussion: endothelial MHC-I and viral infection, and CD8+ T-cell infiltration.

**Ambiguities and caveats**
- **Differential expression:** AD vs NC was tested with a Wilcoxon rank-sum test on nuclei (Seurat FindMarkers), with lenient thresholds (adj P < 0.1, |log2FC| >= 0.1). There is no donor-level model, so the DEG counts are inflated by pseudoreplication.
- **Composition:** no statistical test is reported for any composition change, either at the cell-type level (endothelial 3.0% vs 1.2%) or for subpopulations (a1 +9.9%, a6 +10.2%, a3 -23.5%). It is unclear whether the subpopulation proportions are pooled or per donor.
- **"AD-up/down-regulated" subpopulations:** these were defined by a DEG enrichment score (mean z-score of AD DEGs > 0.5), not by abundance. The claims that oligodendrocyte o3/o5 are "reduced", o1/o2 "increased" and endothelial e1/e3/e4 "induced" (C16, C17, C19) may therefore reflect expression rather than abundance. I coded them as the authors state them and flagged this in each claim's notes.
- **Directions:** the cell-type pathway directions (C04, C07-C10) come from the Discussion summary (impaired synaptic signaling, reduced myelination, aberrant immune activation, enhanced angiogenesis). The Results only say "associated with".
- **Endothelial gene lists:** the abstract names EGFL7, FLT1, VWF, B2M and HLA-E for the angiogenic endothelial cells. The Results name CLDN5, ERG, FLT1, VWF and HLA-E. B2M and EGFL7 do not appear in the Results text.
- **TCR (C23):** this is the T-cell receptor complex, kept as written.
